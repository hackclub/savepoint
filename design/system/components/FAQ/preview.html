Which hours count?
Hours you log in Hackatime on your game from the day Save Point starts. Time from before that, or time another Hack Club program already counted, doesn't.
Can I keep working on my jame gam game?
Yes, that's what Save Point is for. Everything after your jame gam submission counts here.
Can I get more than one prize?
Yes. Approved hours work like money: spend them on any prizes they cover, one of each.
